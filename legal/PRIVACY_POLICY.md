## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create a Foodlings account, we collect
your email address, a password (stored securely by our authentication
provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password in plain text),
and the display name you choose.

**Profile information.** You may optionally add an avatar (chosen from a
built-in set of images — we do not collect a photo of you unless you
attach one to a review, see below), a home city, and an Instagram handle.

**Check-in and purchase history.** When you check in at a partner
restaurant, restaurant staff record the dollar amount of your visit. We
use this to calculate the XP and reward points you earn. We do not
process payments ourselves — you pay the restaurant directly through
their own point-of-sale system, the same way you would without this app.

**Reviews.** If you write a review of a restaurant, we store the review
text, your star rating, and — only if you choose to attach one — a photo.
Attaching a photo requires camera or photo library access, which the app
will ask your permission for at that time; we do not access your camera
or photos for any other purpose.

**Social features.** If you invite or accept a friend connection, we
store that connection so we can show you a leaderboard scoped to people
you're connected with. We do not access your phone's contacts to find
friends.

**Usage data.** We keep records of your reward redemptions, saved deals,
and general app activity to make the app function (e.g., showing your
collection progress).

**What we do not collect.** We do not collect your device's precise
location, access your contacts, or collect any payment card information
— restaurant transactions happen entirely outside the app, through the
restaurant's own payment system.

## How we use your information

We use your information to operate the app: tracking your progress at
restaurants, calculating rewards, showing leaderboards among your
friends, and letting you post reviews. We do not sell your personal
information to third parties.

## Who we share information with

- **Restaurant partners** can see your display name, visit count, and
  the dollar amount of check-ins at their specific restaurant — not your
  full profile, email, or activity at other restaurants.
- **Other users** can see your display name, avatar, review text/photos,
  and — if you've connected as friends — your position on the
  leaderboard and your public profile stats.
- **Service providers.** We use Supabase to host our database,
  authentication, and file storage. Supabase processes data on our
  behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
